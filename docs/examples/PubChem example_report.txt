Next steps (the plugin does not upload anything):
1. Sign in to PubChem Submissions (linked from the PubChem home page). A new account is a test account: you can upload, validate and preview, but nothing becomes public until PubChem upgrades it to a full account.
2. New Submission > Substances > upload the .sdf. In the _pubchem.csv racemates and relative configurations are given as InChI with /s3 or /s2 instead of SMILES; the .sdf is preferred because it keeps the drawn structure.
3. Check the preview of a few records: structure, name, DOI, hold-until date.
4. Commit the submission.
A later upload with the same registry IDs replaces these records (new version); in PubChem Submissions, Substances > Substances In PubChem has Modify and Revoke for deposited records.
